!!! tip "Reusable gallery pattern"
    1. Drop your screenshots into `docs/f1-2025-theme/gallery-images/`.
    2. Replace the placeholder image lines above with your own, e.g.
       `![Race results screenshot](gallery-images/race-results-01.png)`.
    3. Every image inside a `gallery-grid` div is automatically
       click-to-zoom via glightbox — no extra markup needed.

    Some renders come in two layouts — Broadcast and Media — selected via
    the theme options. See [Theme Options](theme-options.md#broadcast-vs-media)
    for details; it's worth adding both variants here once you have
    screenshots.
